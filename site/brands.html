QUESTIONS

GOOD QUESTIONS.

What does it cost?

Your first month is free; you only cover the prizes students win. After that it's a flat monthly fee that we set together with founding partners, and founding partners keep the best rate. We never take a cut of your sales.

Do I need an app, a tablet or any tech?

No. Students show the code on their phone. Your staff look at it, check the live clock is ticking (so it isn't a screenshot), and tap redeem. That's it.

What stops people from cheating?

Each code works once, one per student, and it shows a live clock so a screenshot won't pass. You also set a weekly cap, so you never give out more than you planned.

What if too many students show up?

You set how many codes go out each week and which days and hours they work. When they're gone, your spot rests until next week.

Will this just bring in bargain hunters who never come back?

That's the big problem with coupon sites, and why we built this differently. Students earn your offer by playing a game about their school, they play every day so they see you every day, and the best offers are "free with purchase" or slow-hour deals that bring in paying customers. Your weekly report shows how many come in.

Who plays?

Queen's students. The game is about Queen's, made for Queen's, and promoted through Queen's student channels. We launch at Queen's in January.

Can I change my offer or stop?

Anytime. No contract. Message us and it's changed the same day.

Who's behind Off Campus?
