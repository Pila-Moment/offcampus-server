QUESTIONS

Is it actually free?

Yes. No cost for clubs, now or later.

Are answers anonymous?

Yes. Nobody sees who said what, not even you. Everyone only sees totals ("13 of 21 said yes"), and nothing shows until you release the results. Names only appear on the leaderboard, next to scores.

What kind of questions?

Funny and a little spicy, never cruel. Yes-or-no questions where you guess the club's %, "most popular" picks where you guess the club's top answer, and club trivia. No names, nothing about bodies or anything private.

When do the results come out?

When you say. Your page shows how many have played and a RELEASE button (it unlocks at 5 players). After you release, anyone who hasn't played can still play and get a score straight away.

Do members need an app or account?

No. It's a link. They type a name for the leaderboard and play once.
